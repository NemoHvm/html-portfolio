Who am I?

I'm an architecture student, one that's passionate for both life and art. I think of myself as a very curious person, which is why I'm always looking for new things, experiences and processes. I believe that architecture is a compilation of a variety of individuals with their own knowledge, feelings, experience and coincidences. Because of this, I think architecture is multidisciplinary and should always be made with and open mind and heart, willing to flow with inner and outer thoughts. This is why I'm always eager to learn and embrace new experiences. I try always face new challenges and I'm very excited to face this world filled with different cultures, environments and dilemmas.
